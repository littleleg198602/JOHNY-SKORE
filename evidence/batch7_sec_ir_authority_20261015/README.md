`source_capture`, `index_capture`, `selection`, `knowledge_at`.
`SECIRDisclosureCapture` reuses the exact original-byte4,000,000-byte
capture contract from the person-kind component: record ID, canonical SEC

hidden text, partial paragraphs and URI prefixes cannot establish the fact.
